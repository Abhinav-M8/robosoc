# ROBOSOC

Fusion 360 design portfolio documenting multiple iterations of a RoboSoc competition robot, including drivetrain, ball-control mechanisms, and engineering design evolution.


## Overview
RoboSoc is a robot soccer platform designed and developed in Autodesk Fusion 360 as part of an ongoing engineering design portfolio. This repository documents the complete development journey of multiple robot iterations, showcasing design evolution, mechanical improvements, and engineering trade-offs across different versions.

The project focuses on creating a compact, robust, and competition-oriented robot capable of reliable movement, ball handling, and rapid maneuverability within a RoboSoc-style environment.

#### Competition Purpose:
The objective of the RoboSoc platform is to participate in robot soccer competitions where autonomous or manually controlled robots compete to maneuver a ball and score goals in a confined playing field.

#### Primary goals include:

* [ ] Effective ball possession and control
* [ ]   High maneuverability and acceleration
* [ ]   Structural robustness during collisions
* [ ]  Reliable drivetrain performance
* [ ]   Ease of maintenance and manufacturing
* [ ]   Weight and space optimization
* [ ]   Design Requirements

#### The robot was developed with the following engineering requirements:

    Performance
    Fast acceleration and directional changes
    Stable operation during impacts
    Efficient power transmission
    Consistent traction on arena surfaces
    Mechanical
    Compact chassis structure
    Ease of assembly and maintenance
    Modular subsystem layout
    Manufacturable using commonly available processes
    Competition
    Compliance with RoboSoc size constraints
    Protection of critical components
    Reliable operation during match conditions
    Quick replacement of damaged parts
    Design Evolution

#### Key Mechanisms        
<br>

**Chassis System**   

The chassis forms the primary structural framework of the robot and serves as the mounting platform for all mechanical and electrical subsystems.
The chassis also has carefully crafted dimensions such that it cant be flipped over yet can flip other bots.

**Drivetrain**

The drivetrain is designed to provide efficient power transfer and responsive maneuverability within the competition arena.


Designed to improve ball acquisition, retention, and control during gameplay.
